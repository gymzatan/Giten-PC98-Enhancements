GITEN MEGAMI TENSEI REDUX — START HERE

Extract the entire ZIP, then open START HERE.html in your browser.
The folder contains all three enhancement patches, the control features and the English
manual.

TO INSTALL ALL THREE
1. Open Apply Fixes.html.
   Input: your Japanese PC-98 HDI, or your English-translated HDI.
   Apply, then save Giten_Fixed.hdi.
2. Open Apply Rebalance.html.
   Input: the Giten_Fixed.hdi you just saved.
   Apply, then save Giten_Rebalanced.hdi.
3. Open Apply Windows Artwork.html.
   Input: the Giten_Rebalanced.hdi you just saved.
   Apply, then save Giten_WIN.hdi.
4. Mount and play the final Giten_WIN.hdi. It now contains all three patches.

Pass each newly saved result to the next step. Browser downloads may have a
number added to their filenames; use the newest result you just saved.

OPTIONAL KEYBOARD & GAMEPAD CONTROLS
The controls and battle target prompt/pause are installed or removed together.

Open Apply Controls.html and select the disk you play (for example
Giten_WIN.hdi). Click Install Controls, then save Giten_Controls.hdi.
  Target prompt: while you pick a target in battle, the text box asks
    "Use on whom?" (English) or 「誰に使いますか？」 (Japanese), and battle time stops, so no enemy acts during your pick.
  Keyboard / gamepad controls: the stick walks; the D-pad moves the pointer between menu entries,
    options and targets; X goes to the command panel and back; A and B are
    the mouse buttons. For DOSBox-X, mapper-giten.map sets up an XInput pad
    (see CONTROLS.md).
Apply the control features after the other patches. Remove Controls on the same page removes the complete patch.

OTHER COMBINATIONS
Original combat rules + repairs + new artwork: step 1, then step 3 using its output.
Fixes only: step 1.
Fixes + Rebalance with original artwork: steps 1 and 2.
Artwork only: step 3, selecting your original Japanese or English game HDI.

FOR ENGLISH PLAY
Apply Sneikkimies' English translation to its required Japanese disk first,
then apply Redux to that English result. The translation is a separate download:
https://www.romhacking.net/translations/7343/
Redux retains your selected game's language.

BEFORE PLAYING
Close the emulator before patching. Work on a copy and back up your saves.
The patches read your selected disk and download a separate result locally.
Use the PC-98 emulator and boot setup with which your unmodified copy works.
The PepsimanGB pre-made HDI needs its companion boot floppy: attach both and
boot from the floppy. After changing patches, restart and load an in-game save.

Open Documentation.html for all features, controls, compatibility and help.
